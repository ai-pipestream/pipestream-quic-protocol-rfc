Contract and implementation
  Section 12.6 now explicitly requires the final time check after storage and
  authorization for acquisition, renewal, publication, expansion yield/complete
  and explicit retry. A proposed renewal cannot validate expired prior ownership.
  Newly issued output/receipt intervals must not already have elapsed at commit.
  There is no wire, profile or private storage-format revision.

  Rust captures the original deadline, initial observation and pre-transition
  worker lease before rewriting records. After final authorization it checks
  that interval and persists the final UTC watermark in the same transaction.
  Acquisition checks its proposed lease; renewal checks both old and proposed
  leases; explicit retry checks the original deadline without requiring the
  superseded worker lease. Publication also checks both new retention intervals.
  Existing operation-replay behavior and funded write ordering remain intact.

Reviewed test coverage
  Four new execution/tests.rs tests use exact authorization-call clock hooks.
  Claim refuses equality at its proposed lease expiry; its positive control
  advances final time from 1000 to 1050, persists 1050 and keeps lease end 1100.
  Renewal refuses expiry of the old lease even when the replacement would be
  live; it also refuses an expired shorter replacement while the old lease is
  live. Regression 1070 -> 1060 refuses despite durable watermark 1050.
  Publication covers SUCCEEDED, FAILED and AWAITING_RETRY, exact original
  deadline equality, old lease expiry and exhausted output/receipt intervals.
  Explicit retry refuses a final time at the original deadline without retaining
  its operation or changing the attempt, then accepts the original request once.

  Refusal assertions compare WORK/JOB/CLOCK revision, credits and capacity plus
  durable UTC. Work/job bodies, immutable payload charges and absent receipts
  are checked where relevant. Positive controls prove the ordinary transitions
  still commit. These checks are outside the application callback.

  Two new branch_tests.rs tests each exercise Complete and Yield. Final
  authorization advances to lease equality or the original deadline; refusal
  preserves ACTIVE, the old lease and expansion_complete=false, including on
  AuthorityStore reopen. Within-lease advancement commits the correct distinct
  WAITING_CHILDREN or yielded state without extending the original deadline.
  Reopen here is not a process crash; existing process-death tests ran in the
  full execution family and workspace suite.

  Static review corrected a five-field tuple fixture and strengthened exact
  clock/credit rollback assertions before any test execution. No run-driven
  assertion weakening, build remediation or production change was needed.
