FileInput prehashes a bounded regular file and streams its original commitment
through the durable client. Output::save_to stages until verified FIN, synchronizes
and installs without overwriting a destination. Accepted send/save tasks and file
cleanup retain their ownership through waiter cancellation. The existing fixed
file-worker implementation is reused without changing its scheduling behavior.
No wire/CDDL/storage-format/dependency change, deployment or submission.
