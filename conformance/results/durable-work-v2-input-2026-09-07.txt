ordinary job and one deferred returned-value destructor retain a live input's

ticket. A held actual input preflight proves async cancellation cannot refund
owner quota or complete detach before the file work and stage cleanup end.

buffer is at most 16384 bytes and no larger than the payload chunk ceiling.
