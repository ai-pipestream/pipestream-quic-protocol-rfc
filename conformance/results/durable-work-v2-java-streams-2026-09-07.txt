Scope and limits:
- ClientCorrelation validates a single capability exchange, activated profiles,
  increasing control IDs starting at 1, actual input-stream tags, pending ceilings
  and all response families. It returns correlated controls to the caller; it does
  NOT authenticate peers, check durable receipt fields or persist successful evidence.
- Out-of-order controls/inputs do not require history growing with completed work.
  Inputs remain pending until admission response/refusal or connection uncertainty;
  STOP_SENDING is not an admission receipt. QUIC owns actual stream non-reuse.
- A result header binds generation/work/attempt/index/length/digest to the selected
  retained descriptor. Unknown/non-result/duplicate correlation and a second control
  response after result start poison the connection. Contradictions are local
  INTEGRITY_ERROR even when valid-transfer slots are exhausted. Failed deliveries
  remain pending until abort/FIN/close; releasing one cannot free another's permit.
- Result Bytes/FIN verify the exact payload before completion. Local failure, reset
  or another identical read does not mutate work or advance an execution attempt.
  Close returns unresolved original requests, not terminal work claims.
- HeaderReader checks a u32 prefix before allocating at most 4096 header body bytes,
  preserves coalesced payload for authorization/reservation, and enforces an absolute
  local header deadline. Payload hashes incrementally, rejects size overflow,
  truncation/trailing bytes/digest mismatch and requires actual FIN even when empty.
- Independent idle/lifetime deadline checks can be called without callbacks.
  Deadline equality fails before progress/FIN. Empty callbacks do not renew idle
  time. Signed-long nanoTime wrap works; backward time cannot extend the stream.
- The future Netty owner must drive those timers, gate all incomplete streams/headers,
  bound waiting result creation and queues, enforce actual stream direction/non-reuse,
  preserve control credit and distinguish sender transport progress from queued/file
  bytes. These functions do not establish authenticated endpoint or flow-control
  conformance. Full Java durable execution/recovery, cross-language failure driver
  and original external/equivalent streaming-gRPC workload remain mandatory.
