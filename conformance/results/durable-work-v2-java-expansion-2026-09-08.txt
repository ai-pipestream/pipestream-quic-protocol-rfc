Implemented boundary
  Mode-2 registration requires an actual Expander plus a reassembly callback and
  explicit local producer ceilings. The expander reads the real parent input,
  declares stable producer-one operations, receives/verifies real child bytes and
  admits them through the current-parent-fenced authority transactions. Existing
  receipts and installed inputs are recovered without another child or deadline.
  Local producer limits retain the session's exact durable/result profile choice.

  Complete is a separate funded metadata transition, not a membership seal. It
  requires sealed membership and admitted or already-terminal obligations, then
  moves to WAITING_CHILDREN. Missing seal or unresolved input gives NOT_READY.
  Yield clears the local lease without changing the wire attempt or spending
  settlement credits. Reopen checks the completed expansion and remaining credits.
  Final authorization, trusted UTC and original ownership/deadline still apply.

  Expansion reserves a reusable input receiver credit and its own input reader;
  reassembly reserves the child reader and optional output writer in its later
  invocation. Foreign/closed/borrowed receiver credits refuse. Failed physical
  cleanup retains accounting rather than inventing reusable capacity.
  Unfinished child input cannot complete. Capacity may yield, but subsequent
  thread/ownership/integrity misuse cannot be masked by that earlier refusal.

  Dispatch preserves a ready position when the worker pool is full. Deadline
  discovery has an independent finite cursor. At most one fresh tail extension
  is allowed per dispatch sweep, preventing continuing admissions from indefinitely
  postponing wrap. Per poll: at most one dispatch page, one maintenance page and
  the existing closure batch. This is additional bounded metadata work, not free.

Test meaning and corrections
  New receiver, expansion-transition and runtime tests cover actual byte streams,
  operation replay, named refusals, final-time fences, credits and paired reopen.
  One worker with three storage handles executes two real children, closes their
  scope and publishes the parent's exact reassembled output. Capacity tests admit
  one child, yield on the next, execute the first and resume the same parent attempt.
  Scheduling tests use page sizes 1 and 4; they prove bounded eventual progress in
  those runs, not every possible interleaving. Suffix tests pin fixed upper bounds
  with concurrent admission; occupied-worker deadline tests cross page boundaries.

  external workload and equivalent authenticated durable streaming-gRPC comparison

  not completion, full conformance, deployment, draft submission or IETF adoption.
